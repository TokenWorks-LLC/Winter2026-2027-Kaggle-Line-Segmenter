# Examples

- `images/`: five tablet-face photographs to try `line_segmentation.segment.segment_images` on (see the repository README).
- `line_signs_std_sample/`: a 20-face sample of the `line_signs_std` dataset, with its full description.

The photographs are tablet faces cut from photographs shown in the Electronic Babylonian Library (eBL,
<https://www.ebl.lmu.de/>), which publishes the photographs of its annotated tablets under CC BY-NC 4.0 in the eBL sign
dataset (Lewenstein et al. 2026, <https://doi.org/10.5334/johd.503>). They are shared here under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 for
non-commercial research. Credit the eBL and the holding museum, given by the museum number in each file name:
BM = British Museum, K = British Museum (Kuyunjik collection), HS = Hilprecht Collection (Jena), YBC and NBC = Yale
Babylonian Collection, CBS = Penn Museum, GCBC = Goucher College Babylonian Collection.
